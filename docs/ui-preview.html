Try the demo path

  1. As Priya, open Practice and tap “I practised on my own”. The sun rises and 34 becomes 35.
  2. Switch to the teacher (the round button, top right). In Reports, Priya is now under “Very regular”.
  3. Tap “Invite to Sahaj”, open her full profile, add the tag meditation, and watch the reason change. Send.
  4. Switch back to Priya. The invitation arrives. Tap “Tell me more”.
